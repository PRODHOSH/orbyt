"What's my attendance percentage?"

ORBYT checks your latest academic data and gives you your subject-wise and overall attendance instantly.

"Am I at risk of being debarred?"

ORBYT checks your current attendance against your institution's official attendance and debarment regulations and explains your current status.

ORBYT retrieves the relevant academic regulation and explains the consequences, exceptions, and applicable procedures.

"Which clubs are currently recruiting?"

ORBYT finds active recruitment announcements and highlights opportunities relevant to your interests and skills.

"What do I need to take care of this week?"
